Interests

Outside of writing code, I do two things teaching and soccer. I am constantly thinking about how technology can be used to help bring about understanding in education, as well as make sports, in my case soccer, more fun.

Teaching

I've found that explaining something to someone else is the best test of whether I actually understand it. Teaching has become a big part of my life, both at UCSD and in my community.

Instructional Assistant at UC San Diego

Since September 2026, I've been an Instructional Assistant for CSE 105: Theory of Computability. The course covers finite automata, context-free grammars, Turing machines, and decidability. For many students, it's their first time writing formal proofs.

I hold weekly office hours and lead problem-solving sessions, helping students work through proof techniques for NP-completeness and reduction-based arguments. My favorite part is when I catch a student making the same mistake I used to make and prevent them from falling into the trap.

Catechist at My Parish

I also serve as a catechist at my local Catholic parish on Tuesdays, where I teach faith formation classes to 6th graders. Volunteering there keeps me connected to my community and gives me a very different kind of teaching experience than a computer science classroom. I also help out on Sundays leading table discussions in the OCIA program.

Soccer

I've been a soccer player/fan since the age of 4. I continue to play recreationally. Mostly though, I follow the Premier League closely, which is how I ended up running a Fantasy Premier League group with my friends.

That league even turned into a software project. I built Starboys FPL so we could track the whole season and finally settle who the best manager is.
